## Scope and mode

`/learn/start/decoder`. Visitor mode: Read and locate.

## Audience, job, and action

Readers entering agentic engineering need to translate an unfamiliar term quickly, then understand how adjacent concepts connect. The primary action is locating a term; the secondary action is following its deeper route.

## Content and constraints

Preserve the three thematic clusters, all glossary definitions, analogies, examples, deeper links, amber on-ramp identity, static page shell, and accessible anchor navigation. Do not turn the glossary into a card grid or imply the on-ramp is a fifth Learn pillar.

## Chosen direction

Annotated field lexicon. A compact index establishes all twelve terms at a glance, followed by quiet numbered records with a stable term rail and explicit analogy, definition, and example columns. The memorable moment is the transition from jargon list to a precise conceptual map, not a decorative effect.

## Implementation inventory

- Editorial masthead with term and cluster count.
- Compact three-cluster index with direct term anchors.
- Numbered term records using rules and whitespace instead of cards.
- Clear `Analogy`, `Definition`, and `In practice` labels.
- Desktop alignment that collapses into a single mobile reading sequence.

## Unresolved decisions

None. The user delegated the frontend direction and identified the current route as visually deficient.
