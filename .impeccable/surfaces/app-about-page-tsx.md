## Scope and mode

`/about`. Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, and action

Readers deciding whether Dakota's perspective is credible need to understand the person, practice, trajectory, and working principles without sorting through a project directory. The primary action is continuing to Work or making contact.

## Content and constraints

Use only existing identity, role, focus, foundation, public profiles, and site thesis. Do not claim the Head of AI Innovation title, customer outcomes, employers, education, or biography details not present in repository evidence.

## Chosen direction

Practice profile. A clear personal thesis opens into a concise biography, operating principles, areas of practice, and a direct contact close. Work is removed entirely into its own route. The memorable moment is a large statement of how Dakota approaches change, answered by concrete principles rather than a résumé dump.
